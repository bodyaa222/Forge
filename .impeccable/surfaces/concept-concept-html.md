## Direction contract

THESIS: The number is cast, not printed. Forge's state lives in gym material (raised numerals, knurling on the active set, rack holes for done sessions, safety-yellow paint) and refuses the category default of a black ground with a glowing neon accent.

OWN-WORLD: Three iron values (#18191B ground, #222427 casting, #2B2E32 knurl) plus steel text #ECEAE4 and graphite #9C9EA1; one warm safety yellow #F2C230 used only for state and the primary action, ≤10% of a screen. Unbounded carries every number with a 1px raised highlight; Manrope carries words. 6px machined radius, inset fields, 1px hairlines #34373C.

STORY: The viewer sees the language working before reading rules: today's workout, a record medal, buttons, both tab bars; then every token is justified by an attribute from concept.md.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Live phone frame (390 wide) with the athlete's workout card and logger at left, record medal card and button states at right; athlete and trainer tab bars directly beneath. Primary action «Зберегти підхід» in safety yellow at the bottom of the phone.
